Master reference
The supplied Singularr identity board is included in this folder. The Singularr wordmark and the Requests lettering are traced from that board. The luminous primary symbol is a transparent recreation using built-in ImageGen. The compact symbol and favicon are optical redraws for small sizes.

File use
Display SVG lockups contain embedded raster artwork together with vector lettering. Compact SVGs contain paths only. Files named dark are for dark backgrounds. Black and white are single-ink marks. The ICO contains 16, 32 and 48 px entries. Interface fonts and OFL notices are under Type.

Artwork prompt

Edit the supplied Singularr identity board to isolate ONLY the large detailed black-hole symbol inside the big LEFT panel; the right-hand lockups are references for identity only. Deliver ONE standalone high-resolution transparent PNG logo, wide landscape canvas about 3:2. Match the source artwork as faithfully as possible. Near-circular upright event horizon with a black opaque central pupil, fine lavender and white concentric lensed lines wrapping the dome, and a very wide thin accretion disk sweeping in FRONT of the dark center and tilted upward toward the right by approximately 12 degrees. Same orientation, delicate layered glow, open dark surroundings, luminous ridgeline, and elongated tapered ends as the source. Keep the entire outermost faint filaments inside the canvas and modest even clear space. Remove the card, frame, panel background, layout, all letters and all labels; fully transparent outside and in gaps around the symbol, including outside its halo; maintain the opaque dark core inside the symbol. No new stars, constellations, gradients filling the canvas, or words. No alternative concepts. This is a precise extraction of the selected artwork from the supplied board.
