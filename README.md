<h1 align="center">Singularr</h1>

<p align="center">A self-hosted workspace for Plex media management.</p>

![A luminous violet event horizon beside the words “Escape container sprawl.”](./Brand%20assets/Campaign/Singularr%20launch%20card.png)

## One library, two views

**Singularr** is the workspace for the people who run a Plex library. Movies and shows have separate views. The activity queue identifies the title, edition or episode, responsible service and current state.

**Singularr Requests** is the portal for viewers. Find a movie or show and choose the exact selection you want, down to an individual episode. For example, a request for *Signal Coast* S02E03 names one episode when the owner reviews it.

See both workflows in the [illustrated product and identity guide](./Singularr%20Event%20Horizon%20Guide.pdf).
